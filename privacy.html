Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026. This page describes how the Trade.FlexSpot demo prototype handles information. The demo is a concept build: everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent to our servers.

1. What we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on a server. The demo:

  • Asks only for a display name (no email, no phone number, no password). Your name is stored only in your browser's localStorage so the app can greet you next visit.
  • Stores app preferences and demo activity (theme, open positions, chat messages, poll votes, watchlists) only in your browser's localStorage. It never leaves your device.
  • Sets no cookies. Runs no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no third-party tracking scripts.
  • Does not have an account system, login, or password. The simulated broker-login fields in the demo are purely illustrative — typed values stay in your browser and are never sent anywhere.

2. Optional AI review key

Settings lets you paste your own OpenAI API key to generate an AI review of your simulated trades. That key is stored only in your browser's localStorage; when you request a review, your simulated trade summary and your key are sent directly from your browser to OpenAI's API. We never see, log, or store it. Use it only on devices you trust.

3. Third parties the demo loads

The demo loads public assets from the open internet (e.g. picsum.photos placeholder images) and, in the terminal view, the hosted TradingView chart embed. Those providers may see standard request metadata (IP address, user agent) as with any website visit. We do not share anything else with them because we have nothing to share.

4. Simulated data

All prices, order fills, P/L figures, community posts, followers, likes, chat messages, and live-viewer counts in the demo are simulated or illustrative. They are not real market data and are not financial advice. See our Terms.

5. Your control

Because all demo data lives in your browser, you can erase everything at any time: Settings → Reset demo, or clear your browser's site data. There is no account to delete and nothing on our side to ask us to remove.

6. Children

The demo is a concept prototype, not a financial product, and collects no personal data by design. If you are under the age of majority where you live, use it only with a parent or guardian.

7. Changes

If this prototype becomes a real product with accounts and servers, this policy will be rewritten before any personal data is collected. The policy date at the top will reflect the change.
